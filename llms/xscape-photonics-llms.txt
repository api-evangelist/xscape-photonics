# Xscape Photonics

> Xscape Photonics is a Columbia University silicon-photonics spinout building multicolor laser platforms (FalconX ELSFP laser modules and the EagleX laser chiplet evaluation kit) that relieve GPU-to-GPU and GPU-to-memory bandwidth bottlenecks in AI data center fabrics. The company publishes no public API, SDK, or developer program; this profile documents its web and trust surface for the API Evangelist network.

Backed by NVIDIA, Cisco, Fathom Fund, IAG Capital Partners, and others, with ARPA-E and U.S. Department of Energy SCALEUP program support. Offices in Fort Lee, NJ and Santa Clara, CA.
